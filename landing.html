What you get

Matchmaking is step one.
The workspace is where it ships.

Most platforms stop at “here are some profiles.” SquadUp takes you from zero to a working demo — with a team that didn’t fall apart.

Smart matching

Swipe solo-to-solo for a fresh duo, or request into a moving team. Skills, roles and GitHub — no blind networking.

Live kanban

Idea → PPT → Prototype → Test → Pitch, synced in realtime for the whole team. Deadlines that don’t lie.

Resource hub

Paste any link — Figma, GitHub, Canva, Drive. We detect the domain, show the right badge, and live-preview what’s embeddable.

Mentor corner

Ticket-based help. Mentors see your kanban and resources before they reply — no context-dumping in chat.
